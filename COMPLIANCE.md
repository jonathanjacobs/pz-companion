# Project Compliance

PZ Companion follows the shared Willow Hill Games Project Zomboid mod-development compliance baseline.

Before adding third-party material or preparing any release, review:

- [`docs/PZ_MODDING_POLICY.md`](docs/PZ_MODDING_POLICY.md) — mandatory Indie Stone policy and provenance rules.
- [`docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`](docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) — release gate and publication checklist.
- [`docs/CLEAN_ROOM_POLICY.md`](docs/CLEAN_ROOM_POLICY.md) — stricter clean-room rules for studied third-party implementations.
- [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) — redistributed third-party material and research/prior-art provenance.
- [`ASSET_LICENSE.md`](ASSET_LICENSE.md) — licensing boundary for non-code creative assets.
- [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) — licensing for project-owned source material.

The authoritative external policy is The Indie Stone's Project Zomboid Modding Policy. The live policy must be rechecked before first Steam Workshop publication and periodically thereafter.

Project Zomboid is developed by The Indie Stone. PZ Companion is an independent community mod and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Indie Stone.
