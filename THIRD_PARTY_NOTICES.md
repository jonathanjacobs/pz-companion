# Third-Party Notices

This file records third-party material redistributed with PZ Companion. Research references and prior art that were reviewed but are not redistributed are listed separately so provenance remains explicit.

## Redistributed third-party material

No third-party runtime, model, library, or asset has been selected for distribution yet.

Before a dependency, model, runtime, native library, executable, asset, data file, prompt/content pack, or other third-party component is distributed with the project, record at minimum:

- component/model/asset name;
- version or immutable revision;
- upstream source;
- copyright holder(s) or author(s) where known;
- license or explicit permission basis;
- whether the component was modified;
- redistribution requirements;
- attribution requirements, including Steam Workshop credit where applicable.

A technically compatible component must not be bundled until its redistribution rights are also compatible with the intended Project Zomboid distribution model.

## Research references / clean-room prior art not redistributed

Third-party NPC mods, including **Bandits**, may be studied for behavior, interoperability, API clues, and architectural constraints only under [`docs/CLEAN_ROOM_POLICY.md`](docs/CLEAN_ROOM_POLICY.md).

Unless a specific file is independently proven license-compatible or explicit permission is obtained, their implementation code, names, dialogue, audio, textures, models, artwork, data, and other protected assets must not be copied into PZ Companion.

Listing a project here documents provenance/research history; it does not grant reuse rights.
